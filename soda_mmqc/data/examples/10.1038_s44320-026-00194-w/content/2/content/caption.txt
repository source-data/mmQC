a) Schematic overview of needle sampling in a primary tumor. b) Clonal relationship between the frequency of barcodes detected in primary tumor and needle sample N1 for each model. Each dot represents a barcode, the size of the dot correlates with the frequency of the barcode in the primary tumor. Barcodes present in the tumor center are represented by orange dots, and barcodes exclusively present the periphery are represented by grey dots.
