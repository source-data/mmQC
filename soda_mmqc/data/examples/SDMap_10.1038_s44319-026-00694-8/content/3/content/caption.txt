(A) Weight and cellularity of spleens isolated from 8 to 12-week-old WT and Cmtm6−/− mice. (B) Flow cytometry analysis of splenic T cells from 8 to 12-week-old WT and Cmtm6−/− mice. T cells (gated as CD3+) were subdivided into conventional CD4+ (CD4+, FOXP3-), Tregs (CD4+, FOXP3+), and conventional CD8+ (CD8+). (C) Conventional CD4+ T cells were further gated as naïve (CD44-, CD62L+) and memory (CD44+, CD62L-). (D) Conventional CD8+ T cells were gated as naïve (CD44−), memory (CD44+, CD49d+), and antigen-inexperienced memory T (AIMT) (CD44+, CD49d-) cells. (E–J) Surface FAS levels in the indicated subsets of splenic T cells. Data are presented as mean + SEM, n = 17 WT and 11 Cmtm6−/− mice. Two-tailed Mann-Whitney test. ns not significant.
