Figure 5: Distinct metallochaperone activities of PacL family members.(A, B) Deconvoluted mass spectra obtained by native MS showing cadmium binding to (A) PacL1 but not to (B) PacL1Δ5. (C–F) Proportion of the indicated purified proteins bound to specific numbers of metal atoms, assessed by native mass spectrometry. (G, H) Residue-specific perturbations induced by (G) zinc and (H) cadmium on PacL1. Summary of chemical shift perturbations of PacL1 upon zinc and cadmium interaction at 0.4 (light gray), 1.0 (gray), and 2 (black) equivalents of metal. Black stars indicate peak broadening below detection due to chemical exchange. PacL1: soluble domain of PacL1; PacL1Δ5: soluble domain of PacL1 deleted of its C-terminal metal-binding motif; PacL2: soluble domain of PacL2.
