Model predictions for continuous and alternating treatments. Mathematical model
simulations, parameterized in figure 4, shown for MCF-7 (A, B) and T47-D (C) cell lines,
respectively. Top panels: in silico simulations show spheroid cell count over time for
continuous mono- and combination therapy and alternating/sequential therapy for fast and
slow switching. Bottom panels: in vitro validation of model predictions for spheroid cell count
over time for all therapy regimens. Treatments include chemotherapy concentration of 25nM
