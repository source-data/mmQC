(A–C) Cryo-EM map of the 48S initiation complex assembled on the EMCV IRES, shown in different orientations. The 40S ribosomal subunit, ribosomal proteins uS13 and uS19, associated initiation factors, Met-tRNAiMet and EMCV IRES are shown in distinct colors and are labeled accordingly. (D–G) Zoomed-in views of the EMCV IRES density map (rendered at 70% surface transparency) superimposed with the atomic model, displayed in different orientations. The individual domains of the IRES are indicated in these views. (H–K) Zoomed-in views of the cryo-EM density map, with superimposed atomic models, showing the key interactions between the EMCV IRES (red) and initiator tRNA (magenta), uS13 (blue) and uS19 (green). The densities of the different components are color-coded using the ChimeraX surface color tool (Meng et al, 2023). Panel I also shows interaction between the start codon (AUG) of the mRNA and the anti-codon of the initiator tRNA at the P site of the 40S subunit.
