A) Schema of CCR5-tropic HIV-1 strain (NLAD8) infection in BRGS mice (done with BioRender). B) Graphs showing viral infectivity in BRGS mice by quantitative RT PCR, measuring the late reverse transcripts copies per mL of blood in each mouse(left) (Data are shown as the mean ± SD of two replicates), quantification of the percentage of cells presenting CPSF6 clusters per mouse (middle) and the number of CPSF6 clusters per nuclei in individual mice (right) (the n corresponds to the number of cells analysed).
